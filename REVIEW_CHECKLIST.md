# Review checklist for CUK COD and actuarial expert

Please assess:

1. Is the research problem sufficiently focused and relevant to actuarial science?
2. Is the proposed rainfall index appropriate for the selected crop and local growing season?
3. Are the rainfall reference period, trigger and exit level justified?
4. Is the historical dataset authoritative, representative and sufficiently long?
5. Are missing values, spatial resolution and data quality addressed?
6. Is the pure-premium / burn-cost method appropriate as an initial estimate?
7. What additional risk loadings or actuarial methods are required?
8. How should basis risk be measured against observed crop losses or yields?
9. What validation design and out-of-sample tests are required?
10. What ethical, regulatory and data-governance issues should be addressed before any farmer-facing pilot?

The reviewer is invited to recommend revisions before the project is presented as a validated model.
